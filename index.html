Our Craft

Handmade, not mass-produced

Huxley Jewelry Creations began the way most good things do — with a small bench, a few hand tools, and a stubborn belief that jewelry should be made for a person, not a shelf. We still work the same way today.

Every order starts with a conversation. We talk through the metal, the weight, the size and the finish, sketch the piece so you can see it before it exists, then handcraft it in sterling silver or gold — shaped, soldered, set, filed and polished by hand. Because nothing leaves the bench until we would wear it ourselves.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do you really handcraft “any kind” of jewelry?

Yes. Rings, pendants, college rings, earrings, bangles, bracelets and chains are what we are asked for most, but if you can describe it or show us a photo of it, we can handcraft it — including one-of-a-kind pieces and matching sets.

How much does a custom piece cost?

It depends on the metal, the weight and the stones — a simple silver pendant and a gold ring set sit in very different ranges. Tell us your design and your budget, and we will send an honest quote before any work begins. No charge for the design consultation.

How long does an order take to make?

Simple pieces are usually finished within a few days; detailed or stone-set pieces take longer. Wedding and engagement rings should be ordered as early as possible, especially during busy months. Let us know your date and we will confirm the timeline up front.

Can you copy a design I saw online or resizing my old ring?

Send us the photo or bring the piece in. We can create something in the same spirit, adjust a design to your size and budget, or repair, resize and re-polish jewelry you already own.

Do you do group or bulk orders?

Yes — class rings, bridal party sets, corporate giveaways and family tokens are all welcome. Message us with the quantity and your target date for group pricing.

How do I pay, and how do I receive my order?

We confirm payment details with you once your design and quote are approved. Finished pieces can be picked up from us or shipped nationwide and worldwide; we will send tracking details once it is on the way.
