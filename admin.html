How this works

Confirmed and pending appointments are hidden from clients automatically. Use the blockers on the left only for time you are unavailable for other reasons.

Marking the reservation fee Paid is what records the ₱1,000 in Fees collected. Use Deducted once it has been applied to a finished ring order. Bookings you have not collected from yet stay at ₱0.

Website photos Changes appear on the site within seconds

How to change a photo

Upload — press the button, pick a photo from your phone or computer. It is resized and compressed automatically, so straight-from-camera files are fine. Landscape photos (wider than tall) look best.

Or paste a link — if the photo is already online (a Facebook post, your Google Drive, a supplier's page), paste its address and press Save. The link must start with https://.

Reset returns a slot to the original design photo.

Change username or password

Your password is stored salted and hashed on Google's side. Nobody can read it from the website files.

Good to know

Who can see this page? Anyone can open the address, but without the username and password nothing loads. All the checking happens on the server — pressing Ctrl+U shows no credentials.

Where is the data? In your own Google Sheet. This portal is only a window into it, so you can always open the sheet directly on your phone.

Back it up now and then: open the sheet → File → Make a copy.
